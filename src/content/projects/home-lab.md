My home lab started as a way to stop depending on cloud services for things I use every day. It also allowed me to go deeper into learning more about virtualization, network, and security. This page is just a working record of it and will change as the lab changes.


## Hardware

- Beelink Mini PC
- Raspberry Pi 4B
- Raspberry Pi 5
- Mac Mini

## Services

- Home Assistant — this allows me to control non-HomeKit IoT devices with Apple's HomeKit like Nest and other non-native Home devices.
- CasaOS — A dashboard homepage for everything.
- Manyfold — An offline library for my 3D print files.
- Syncthing — Keeps my Obsidian notes synced across devices without a cloud service.
